RTL8711DAF (Realtek AmebaDplus) flash loader (vendor binary, "floader").

Taken unmodified from Realtek AmebaImageTool, Devices/Floaders/floader_amebadplus.bin,
as shipped in the TuyaOS rtl8711daf vendor SDK
(rtl8711daf_os/tools/ameba/ImageTool, ChangeLog up to v2.8.10 / 2025-05-27).
Do not confuse it with the 32000-byte ImageTool/Floaders/floader_amebadplus.bin
next to it: that older image speaks the legacy protocol and is not this one.

The vendor ships no version number for the image itself. It starts with the Ameba
image signature 99 99 96 96 3F CC 66 FC, declares 0x6140 bytes loaded at
0x3000A020, entry 0x3000B819. The 1.0.0 above is tyutool's own asset version.

29024 bytes; uploaded through the mask-ROM UART download mode (xmodem-style STX
packets, followed by the transfer baud as a u32 LE and zero padding) to 0x3000A000
and entered there.
