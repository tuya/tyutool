Batch Flash & Auth

Plug multiple devices into your computer's serial ports and flash firmware + write auth codes in parallel, driven by an .xlsx spreadsheet. This is the most complex feature in tyutool and involves irreversible eFuse operations.

This feature serves two very different audiences, and we provide two separate guides. Pick the one that matches who you are:

I'm a firmware developer

What UART commands must my firmware implement to be batch-authorizable? Full protocol contract and a self-test checklist.

I'm a flashing operator

How to prepare, how to operate, and what to do when something breaks. Operations only, no technical details.
The single most important safety note If you select OTP storage mode (T5AI only), credentials are burned into the chip once and can never be erased or modified — one wrong setting ruins the whole batch. Always validate on a single device before going to batch. See Operator Guide · Safety.
